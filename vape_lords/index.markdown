## About

Vape Lords started as a much simpler idea for a clicker / idle game, and has grown into a lightweight desktop RPG with travel, encounters, equipment, shops, progression, persistent state, and an increasingly strange world built around vapor and scavenged technology.

The design changes often. I prototype mechanics, play with them, simplify them, replace them, and occasionally remove them entirely as the game takes shape.

## Selected Systems

- Persistent game state and save/load
- Travel, locations, encounters, and combat
- Equipment, inventory, character progression, and economy
- All data human-readable and editable through simple a organized spreadsheet with support for variables
- Shops, scavenging, drops, and upgrade experiments
- Controller navigation & rumble, Steam Deck usability
- Unified game-event and message-output systems

Combat has gone through several major revisions as I work toward something tactical enough to be interesting without losing the game's absurd tone.

## AI-Assisted Development

ChatGPT and Windsurf IDE are a major part of the development workflow. I use them for implementation, debugging, refactoring, codebase analysis, and quickly trying different approaches to a design problem.

I remain responsible for the game design, technical direction, testing, acceptance criteria, UX decisions, regression checking, and deciding which approaches stay in the project.
